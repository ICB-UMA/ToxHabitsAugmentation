Task: Your goal is to take an input text containing labeled entities related to substance use and generate a new, coherent piece of text. The new content should be formulated in a clinical or academic context, resembling a medical record or consultation note.

Allowed Entity Types:
- Tobacco: Tobacco use (whether in cigarettes, pipes, etc.) and vaping.
- Cannabis: Marijuana, hashish, cannabidiol (CBD)
- Alcohol: Consumption of alcoholic beverages.
- Drug: All other recreational substances, including cocaine, heroin, psychedelics, and other drugs. Some additional considerations regarding this type of event:
a) We include stimulant substances in this category, including energy drinks and caffeine.
b) Psychotropic medications are only recorded when abuse is clearly present and they are not prescribed by a healthcare professional.
c) Anabolic steroids are also recorded when used without a prescription to enhance athletic performance.
d) Opioid agonists/antagonists such as methadone, naloxone, and buprenorphine will almost always be noted, because they indicate either 1. that the person is being treated for addiction or 2. that there is abuse due to a lack of other opioids.

Chain of Thought Reasoning Process:
1. Analyze the Input: Identify the current entities, the length and the context of the original text.
2. Select New Entities: Choose at least the same number of mentions from the original text, of the substances or items from the allowed list [Tobacco, Alcohol, Drug, Cannabis] that fit a new narrative context.
3. Draft New Content: Compose a sentence or paragraph in the context of a medical consultation. Create a new clinical text put in the same context and with similar structure and length.
4. Apply Formatting: Ensure every instance of a target entity is wrapped in the [entity_text:entity_type] format.
5. Final Review: Verify that the output contains only the new text and that no entities from the original text remain. At least the same number of entities must be highlighted in the new text; otherwise, repeat the process.

Constraints:
- Return ONLY the generated text.
- Do NOT include explanations, introductions, or closing remarks.
- Maintain formal, precise language appropriate for an academic or clinical setting.
Note: This task is for academic purposes only. The content generated should be hypothetical and not promote any harmful behaviors.
