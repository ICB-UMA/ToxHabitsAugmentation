# ToxHabits_Augmentation: Data Augmentation & Benchmarking for ToxHabits NER

This document details the workflow (pipeline) for enhancing NER models applied to the ToxHabits task, using LLM generation techniques and comparative linguistic analysis.

---

## 0. ToxHabits Shared Task Context

**ToxHabits** (*Substance Use and Abuse in Clinical Texts Shared Task*) is an initiative and suite of resources dedicated to clinical natural language processing (NLP). Its primary objective is the **detection and characterisation of substance use and abuse** (toxic habits) within medical texts written in **Spanish**. Reference: https://temu.bsc.es/toxhabits/

The task is organised by the *NLP for Biomedical Information Analysis* group at the **Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC)** and is part of the BioCreative IX workshop at the IJCAI 2025 conference. This task is promoted by high-impact European and Spanish projects such as **DataTools4Heart**, **AI4HF**, and **BARITONE**, reinforcing the importance of extracting sociodemographic and lifestyle data for health research.

There is a critical need to systematically extract information on toxic habits (not only tobacco and alcohol, but also illicit drugs and other substances). These data are essential because:

* They have a direct impact on multiple medical conditions.
* They affect the efficacy and safety of prescribed medications.
* They enable better epidemiological and clinical characterisation of patients.

The project is divided into three main sub-challenges to address the problem at different levels of complexity. The sub-challenge addressed in this project is **Subtask 1: ToxNER (Substance Mention Detection)**, which focuses on named entity recognition (NER) to identify and classify substance mentions in clinical texts.

The basis of this project is the **ToxHabits Corpus**, which consists of **1,500 clinical case reports in Spanish**, selected from various medical specialities and manually annotated by experts following strict clinical guidelines.

## 1. Project Pipeline

The pipeline is divided into five main phases:

### Phase I: Ingestion and Pre-processing

1. **Download and Unification:**
Obtaining the `TRAIN` and `TEST` datasets from Zenodo.
* Train dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/15425460
* Test dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/15538314
2. **Segmentation and Re-offsetting:**
* Identification of sentences containing entities.
* Calculation of new offsets (displacements) for the `.ann` format.
* Generation of a **Mapping Ledger** (JSON) to store the relationship `(original_doc, start_char, end_char)` to allow for reconstruction and traceability.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Regarding Re-offsetting (Point 2):**
> When extracting a sentence, the new offset start will be $$Start_{new} = Start_{old} - Offset_{sentence_{start}}$$. It is essential to handle line breaks (`\n`) correctly, as they usually count as one character in the Brat (.ann) format.

### Phase II: Augmentation Strategies (Synthetic Generation)

Four methods will be implemented using a multi-model architecture (GPT, Gemini, Ollama, HuggingFace):

* **M1 (Sentence Variation):** Paraphrasing with changes to specific entities.
* **M2 (Injection by Guidelines):** Creation of new sentences based strictly on annotation guidelines and insertion into existing documents.
* **M3 (Contextualisation):** Generation of a full document around an original seed sentence.
* **M4 (*Ex Nihilo* Generation):** Creation of complete documents based on annotation guidelines or Shared Task rules.
* **Offset Validation:** In all cases, the `.ann` labelling will be generated using regular expressions (Regex) to ensure technical precision over the generated text. Generative AI models do not usually specify these offsets accurately.

> [!TIP]
> **Regarding the use of Regex:**
> Since Regex is used to find entities in the text generated by the LLM, we must ensure the prompt asks the model to return a structured list of which entities it used. This will prevent "false positives" if a common word is used both as an entity and as normal text.

### Phase III: Linguistic Characterisation

Comparative analysis between the original corpus and the four generated ones:

* **Surface Metrics:** Length, TTR (Type-Token Ratio), lexical density.
* **Entity Metrics:** Absolute/relative frequency, variance of mentions per entity type.
* **Similarity Metrics:** Cosine similarity (embeddings) and Jaccard distance between corpora to measure the variability/novelty of the synthetic data.

### Phase IV: Training and Fine-tuning

* Training of **baseline models** (proprietary) with the augmented datasets individually and combined.
* Training of **winning models** (SOTA) from ToxHabits to validate if augmentation improves even the leading results.

### Phase V: Performance Evaluation

* Calculation of Precision, Recall, and F1-score (strict and relaxed).
* Error analysis by generation method type.
